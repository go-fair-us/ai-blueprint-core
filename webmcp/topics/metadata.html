Minimal metadata schema

A schema.org-based minimum for describing digital objects. Repositories choose how they store the values.

Metadata is the context that makes an object findable. When the bits themselves cannot be fully shared (personal health information, very large files), the metadata can still be exposed. The Blueprint lists a minimum set rather than a rigid, all-or-nothing schema. Adopting even a subset still supports alignment.

Capture belongs at deposit time, with researchers documenting the same fields while they create the object. Other schemas are acceptable when they interoperate with schema.org, are openly maintained, and are in wide use.
