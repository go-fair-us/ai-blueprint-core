# Prompt

You are helping a NIAID-supported data repository fulfill the 'Point of Contact for Outreach and Training' requirement in the Blueprint.

Repository: ImmuneSpace — homepage and data access portal for the Human Immunology Project Consortium (HIPC) (https://immunespace.org/), established by the NIAID Division of Allergy, Immunology, and Transplantation. ImmuneSpace provides curated human immune-profiling studies (vaccination, infection, medication, immune-mediated disease), linked resources from ImmPort and GEO/SRA, immune signatures, data standards, modules, and a community discussion forum. Peer NIAID resources for interoperability context: ImmPort Shared Data (https://www.immport.org/), IEDB (https://www.iedb.org/), and VEuPathDB pathogen portals (https://veupathdb.org/).
Current team size and skills:
- Small-to-medium core: HIPC Coordinating Center staff plus contributions from ~8 US HIPC centers (e.g., Benaroya, Columbia, Mount Sinai, La Jolla Institute for Immunology, MIT, Seattle Children’s, Stanford, Yale — as listed on ImmuneSpace)
- Skills mix: human immunology domain experts, multi-omics / systems immunology analysts, data curators familiar with ImmPort study models, web/portal engineers, and community managers for discuss.immunespace.org
- Existing outreach assets: documentation and user guides on the discussion forum (site features, data standards, announcements); publication tracking (~1300+ related publications); open data access UI with filters on sample types, experiments, and demographics; HIPC data standards materials
- Constraints: keep the plan realistic for a small-to-medium team that already juggles multi-center data integration; designate a single Blueprint-facing point of contact without inventing a large new training department

Create a lightweight, practical plan that includes:
- Who should be the designated point of contact
- What minimal training/outreach activities they should offer
- How this supports the NIAID Data Ecosystem Discovery Portal

Keep it realistic for a small to medium team.
