Major repositories should designate a Contact Point for outreach, training, and Portal collaboration, with prominent contact information and community training materials that include user engagement support.

See also: [Motivation](/outreach-training/motivation.md).

# Atomic concepts

| # | Concept | Lines |
|---|---------|-------|
| 173 | Major NIAID data repositories should designate a Contact Point (CP) for outreach, training, and collaboration with the NIAID Data Ecosystem Discovery Portal. | 255 |
| 174 | The CP should engage with NIAID and other stakeholders. | 255 |
| 175 | The CP should participate in NIAID Data Ecosystem Discovery Portal training and outreach activities. | 255 |
| 176 | The CP should help facilitate the transfer of key information to repository staff to support NIAID Blueprint implementation. | 255 |
| 177 | Repositories will be included in identifying time commitments and training formats appropriate for their resources. | 255 |
| 178 | The CP should be reachable to answer questions about data or data access procedures by researchers in the community. | 257 |
| 179 | Contact information for an individual or group in the CP role should be listed prominently on relevant websites. | 257 |
| 180 | The same CP should support the NIAID Data Ecosystem Discovery Portal training and outreach activities to ensure continuity and responsiveness. | 257 |
| 181 | Data repository owners are responsible for designating one person to serve as CP, but are also welcome to additionally list a group alias for an outreach, community management, or training group. | 257 |
| 182 | Generators and repositories of NIAID digital objects should develop training materials for the community, detailing how to effectively use the digital objects provided. | 259 |
| 183 | Training materials should include contact information to support user engagement and resource adoption. | 259 |
