What the Blueprint is

A minimal shared specification for NIAID digital objects, not a complete repository design.

NIAID-funded research produces millions of digital objects: data, software, methods, and workflows for infectious and immune-mediated disease research. Findability still fails when standards, access rules, licenses, and metadata differ from one repository to the next.

The Office of Data Science and Emerging Technologies (ODSET) worked with GO FAIR US and published the NIAID Blueprint for Digital Objects. A digital object here is a sequence of bits with a persistent identifier and metadata.

Five areas

  1. Minimal metadata schema — required NIAID-relevant elements on a schema.org basis, with default formats.
  2. Persistent identifiers — PIDs for objects, people, organizations, and selected scientific terms.
  3. APIs for metadata — expose those elements through a small, open access pattern.
  4. Citation — how digital objects should be cited, with PIDs in the citation text.
  5. Outreach and training — a named contact and materials so people can actually use the objects.

Implementing this set is meant to improve discovery and reuse, and to speed work on countermeasures against infectious and immune-mediated diseases.
