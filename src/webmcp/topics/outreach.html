Outreach and training

A named Contact Point and public training materials are part of the Blueprint, not extras.

Shared schemas and APIs do not help if nobody knows how to deposit, cite, or retrieve objects. Major NIAID repositories should designate a Contact Point (CP) for outreach, training, and work with the NIAID Data Ecosystem Discovery Portal.

Contact Point

  • Engage with NIAID and other stakeholders.
  • Join Discovery Portal training and outreach so there is one responsive face for the repository.
  • Pass Blueprint implementation details to repository staff.
  • Answer community questions about data and access procedures.
  • List an individual (required) and, if useful, a group alias on public pages.

Training materials

Generators and repositories should publish materials that explain how to use their digital objects, and those materials should include contact information. Time commitments and formats can be chosen with the repository, rather than imposed as a single course.
