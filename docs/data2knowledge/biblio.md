## Named in the deck, without a link

These are standards, specifications, and profiles the slides or notes cite by name. They are in first-appearance order. A locator is included only where it was checked for this list, and each of those is marked as added. The deck itself does not contain these addresses.

- W3C Text and Data Mining Reservation Protocol (TDMRep).
- ODRL (Open Digital Rights Language), paired in the notes with DUO codes.
- DUO (Data Use Ontology).
- FAIR Signposting `Link` headers. The URL is item 30, on slide 27.

- `cr:citeAs`, described in the notes as the BibTeX cite-as link.

**Slide 11, speaker notes:**

- Keller, P., and M. Thomson, ed. “A Vocabulary For Expressing AI Usage Preferences.” IETF Internet-Draft, `draft-ietf-aipref-vocab-07`, 19 August 2026. The notes name this draft and the date. Locator added: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-aipref-vocab-07
- IETF AI Preferences attachment draft. The notes say it adds a `Content-Usage` rule to `robots.txt` and that the same preference can be sent as an HTTP header. The example they quote is `Content-Usage: train-ai=n`. They do not give a draft number. The August 2026 draft that defines that header and that robots.txt rule is “Associating AI Usage Preferences with Content in HTTP,” `draft-ietf-aipref-attach-05`, 19 August 2026. Locator added: https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-ietf-aipref-attach-05.html
